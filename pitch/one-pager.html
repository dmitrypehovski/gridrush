how it learns
proposer → plan → 8,760-hour verifier → score → keep if better. Two proposers: a 9-parameter policy mutator, then a per-site local search where every proposal is judged by the full-year simulation. The best plan is then rewritten by an LLM agent that writes policy code: 7 generations, one recorded failure, one jump from 178.6 to 279.9. The policy also repairs itself — shrink MW until every site's own SLA holds.
physics check (real AC power flow)
pandapower mv_oberrhein: a real 20 kV German feeder, 179 buses, 2×25 MVA. Base case converges at min V 0.9756 p.u. with 0 violations. The feeder has 8.2 MW of spare capacity; our five largest sites need 31.8 MW — they cannot share one feeder. This is a siting problem, not a megawatt problem: 0.5–10 MW edge sites are the right unit.
Inside the loop: with the measured 6.53 MW per feeder as a hard admission constraint, the same agent gets 2.39× instead of 4.30× (net $155M vs $280M); all 8 substations pass real AC PF. The delta is the value of real utility ICA data.
